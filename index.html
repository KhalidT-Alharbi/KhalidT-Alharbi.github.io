OnKith

Training data · Team of six · KAUST Academy · 2026

A privacy-first voice assistant that runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 and masks personal information before anything leaves the device.

  • Python
  • NLP
  • PII detection
  • Hugging Face
Website ↗ Repository ↗ Dataset ↗
Details

The PII detector kept missing Saudi names. The team agreed it needed Saudi data, and I built it: a generator that draws on official Saudi sources, with correct SAMA IBAN and 15-digit ZATCA VAT formats, plus extra rows for the entity types the detector scored worst on.

I then assembled and published the full 284,619-row training corpus: seven sources, 31 entity types, and leakage-aware splits so a model cannot be validated on rows that are near-copies of what it trained on.

The detector trained on it reaches 0.84 private-character recall on a frozen out-of-distribution test set, which the team held back from training, augmentation and model selection. That generation changed the recognizer, the label set and the data together, so the corpus is one of three reasons the number moved.

HI-BSDS

Firmware · Team of six · KAU engineering design · 2026

A helmet-mounted blind spot warning system for motorcycle riders, built so the protection moves with the rider instead of being wired into one bike.

  • C++
  • ESP32
  • mmWave radar
  • LiDAR
Details

Each side of the helmet has its own ESP32 pod reading a 24 GHz mmWave radar and an 8x8 time-of-flight LiDAR. An alert only fires when the radar confirms the target is moving, and the in-helmet LEDs turn yellow inside 4 m, orange inside 2.5 m and red inside 1.2 m.

Each pod reads its radar at 256,000 baud and its LiDAR at 921,600 baud, then sends a compact status line to the hub twenty times a second. A colour only changes after it holds for three frames, so a single bad reading cannot make the LEDs flicker.

A third board, an ESP32-S3, streams the camera over its own Wi-Fi network with each side's threat level overlaid on the video. Fitting three sensor streams onto one chip meant moving the debug console to USB and remapping a hardware serial port to free it for the rangefinder. I wrote the firmware for all three boards.
